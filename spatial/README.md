# Spatial Design 009 — 1AGE Shop
Independent walkable global marketplace preview at spatial/index.html. Open to conventional businesses, blockchain businesses and independent creators. Shop is third-party commerce; Store is first-party official 1AGE retail. Blockchain is optional infrastructure, not a prerequisite for sellers or customers.

Five stations: global marketplace, physical goods, digital goods, seller center, and virtual storefronts. Synthetic catalog supports category, product type and fulfillment filters. Local fictional listing form validates basic physical/digital fulfillment compatibility. No actual product, seller, price, order, account or inventory record is created. No live checkout, wallet access, payment or publication.

Future shared data: verified seller identity, product/variant, licensed media, availability, delivery, merchant-owned catalog source, property/virtual space ID, consent, order, refund, dispute, tax and provenance. A single business may connect physical and virtual storefronts through Space without duplication. Normal fiat checkout through suitable regulated partners; optional external crypto via Exchange and approved first-party Cash utility only after review. No private personal/order information on public chains. Blockchain proofs of ownership or authenticity must be independently verified and not substitute for merchant or product verification. Seller eligibility and product moderation, local law, consumer protection, tax, fulfillment and payment review required.

Reuses local Three.js, Earth, logo and gamepad assets with shared Quest walking/architecture pinned at 5d43fd6a3cfc10616cc22cf41ef6264ff946f4f5. Browser, mobile, controller, rendered visual review and graphical parity with HQ1 are unverified. No existing production files or domain settings changed. No merge without approval.
